Mikan Genome Project: exploring Japanese citrus with AI

Biomedical

Public PacBio HiFi data now covers 18 citrus cultivars including Satsuma mandarin, Iyokan, Hassaku, Hyuganatsu, Ponkan and orange, and 2026 resources make haplotype-resolved, allele-level work possible. Can that data support a Japanese citrus pangenome that explains what makes each variety different?

  • Build a haplotype-aware, Japanese citrus-focused pangenome from public HiFi data
  • Bring in experience with pangenomes and allele phasing — ideas and interpretation especially welcome
  • Run the large analyses on the OASIS HPC system at Kyushu University MIB

Team: Shuto Machida, Toshiaki Katayama, Mayumi Kamada
