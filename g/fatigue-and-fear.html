When Fatigue Reveals Fear

Others

When landing on both feet, do people considered recovered still shift away from the injured side, does fatigue amplify that shift and destabilise the trunk and pelvis, and does fatigue make fear of movement legible in the body? The hypothesis: conscious control masks psychological state at rest, and fatigue removes the mask.

  • Show with open data that readiness to return to sport is a psychological state as much as a mechanical one
  • Test whether axial stability under fatigue tracks psychological readiness as closely as knee function does

Team: Keitaro Takaoki, Haruma Abe, Teppei Okazaki
