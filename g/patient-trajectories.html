Patient Trajectory Matching over Temporal Knowledge Graphs

Biomedical

Clinical trajectories are how diagnoses, treatments, procedures and measurements unfold over time. An open trajectory query engine would find patients whose histories satisfy a temporal pattern, then widen the search through explicitly permitted semantic and temporal relaxations, showing the evidence behind every result.

  • Develop a theory of what a temporal knowledge graph actually is
  • Extend SULO for temporal knowledge representation
  • Specify a compact, flexible query language and implement indexed exact and fuzzy retrieval
  • Demo against MIMIC-IV: acute-care episodes involving medication exposure and later changes in kidney function

Team: Michel Dumontier, Orion Buske, Robert Hoehndorf, Chang Sun
