Connecting NANDO with MedGen

Biomedical

Linking Japanese rare and intractable disease information to international biomedical resources, by identifier and then by label.

  • Explore existing links between NANDO and external disease resources
  • Identify MedGen IDs through linked identifiers and generate candidate mappings
  • Use LLMs to compare disease labels and synonyms where identifiers do not resolve
  • Classify the results into matches, ambiguous cases and non-matches

Team: Terue Takatsuki, Evan Bolton, Toyofumi Fujiwara, Masae Hosoda
