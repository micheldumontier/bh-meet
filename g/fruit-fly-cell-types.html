Let fruit fly do novel cell type detection

Others

The whole-brain fly connectome has been published as MaleCNS v1.0. Flies lose interest in a smell after repeated exposure — so turn single-cell transcriptome profiles into smells, let a simulated fly experience as many as possible, and see whether it stays calm for a known cell type and reacts to a novel one.

  • Encode single-cell transcriptome profiles as olfactory stimuli
  • Train the in-silico fly brain on known cell types
  • Test whether novelty shows up as excitation
