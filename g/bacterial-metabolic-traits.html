Predicting bacterial metabolic traits from genomes using AI

Others

Predicting the metabolic traits of bacteria — the electron donors and acceptors they use for energy metabolism, and their energy metabolism pathways — from genome annotation, and checking AI curation against manual curation.

  • Review research papers with AI and extract the data
  • Compare AI curation with manual curation
  • Annotate predicted proteins with KofamScan and convert the results to RDF
  • Predict metabolic traits through MCP and visualise on phylogenetic trees

Team: Shuichi Kawashima, Yoko Okabeppu, Takeru Nakazato, Risa Otsuka, Julia Koblitz
