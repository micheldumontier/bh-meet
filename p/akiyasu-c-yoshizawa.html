Akiyasu C. Yoshizawa

Niigata University · Japan

Once an experimental biophysicist in membrane biology, then bioinformatics at Kyoto University and AIST — comparative genomics of eukaryotic intracellular transport, and assembling in-house sequence databases. Mass spectrometry at the University of Tokushima and Shimadzu. Now one of the developers of jPOST, a proteome raw data repository, and MB-POST, a metabolome repository in the Shin-MassBank project, mainly developing analysis and statistical methods and preparing metadata.

Topics: Proteomics, Metabolomics, Databases, Genomics
