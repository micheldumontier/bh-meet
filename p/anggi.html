Biomedical and Genome Science Initiative (BGSI) · Indonesia

Bioinformatics lead at BB Binomika, part of BGSI, Indonesia's national whole genome sequencing programme under the Ministry of Health. Also benchmarked MGI against Illumina on 23 dually sequenced samples.
