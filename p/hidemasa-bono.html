Hidemasa Bono

Hiroshima University / DBCLS · Japan

Professor of bioinformatics and genome informatics at Hiroshima University and a member of DBCLS since 2007. Initiator of TogoTV, RefEx and FANTOM, PI for the BioDX centre of innovation (COI-NEXT), and deputy director of the HU Genome Editing Innovation Center. Works on data-driven genome breeding, the data analysis environment for genome editing, and gene function analysis.
